:::lead
Prof Peter Ng explicitly dislikes being celebrated. He would reinstate himself just to fire us if we did that. Instead, this piece builds his portrait entirely through the voices of colleagues and friends, letting specific memories (a stonefish injury, a hat lost at sea, a bag of crabs) do what a list of achievements never could: show the kind of man he is.
:::
